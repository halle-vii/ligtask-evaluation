Export metric data

Includes all seven models, separated by model. Exports every prompt matching the current filters, across all pages.

A model's results always come from one run. Missing models stay in the export with blank results; no new evaluations are started.

CSV: numerical inputs only, including model codes, binary labels, safety ratings, and response status. Query text, context text, explanations, and raw responses are excluded.

ZIP: a CSV per model, a combined CSV, and a codebook describing the numerical codes and source runs.

Human classifications come from gold.label and are exported as 0/1. Unrecognized or missing labels stay blank. Human mean safety scores come from gold.rating_mean. Positive-class probabilities are model self-reported estimates from new evaluations; legacy or missing values stay blank.
